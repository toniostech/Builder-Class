Nominator pools exist because solo validation requires a minimum stake of three hundred thousand Gram plus the technical ability to run a high-performance server around the clock. Most holders can’t meet either bar. A nominator pool is a smart contract that combines smaller deposits — recommended minimum ten thousand Gram per person, up to forty nominators per pool — behind one validator. The validator runs the node and takes a commission; the rest of the rewards are split proportionally. If the validator gets slashed, penalties come out of the validator’s balance first. This lets ordinary holders earn staking rewards and help secure the network without operating infrastructure themselves.
